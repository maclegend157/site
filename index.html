About

Hi, I'm Mac.

Bringing people together is the thing I'm best at. I live in Downtown San Diego, and I'm consistently dating new women. I've been in the dating game since I was 18. I'm 31 now, so that's 13 years of experience. I host RATED, a dating show we film right here downtown.

I've spent years getting good at this. I started with the pickup stuff and cold approach, and I got good at it. I still have those skills. But I outgrew it, because it's slow, painful, and the ROI on my hours and dollars spent was arguably low. Hours out every night for a handful of numbers, and most of them go nowhere. I wanted a way to meet better women in a fraction of the time, so dating fit into my life instead of taking it over. So I learned what actually works. I'm the oldest of four with three sisters, I'm close with my mom, and the women I date tell me I understand women better than most guys do.

Online was a different story. For years, dating apps didn't work for me. They sucked. I'd get one match every three days, and she'd be a 6.5 at best. Then I figured out how to fix my game online, on the apps and on Instagram, and everything changed.

I've been on thousands of dates with hundreds of women. I see the patterns. More importantly, I've gotten a lot of real-world feedback from women themselves about what does and doesn't work. That's what I teach you.

I started coaching because guys kept asking me for help. Shy guys. Guys fresh off a breakup. Guys who'd never had a girlfriend. Guys who looked great on paper and still went home alone every night. This is private. I take a very small number of men at a time, and I'm in it with you until your dating life looks the way you decide it should look.
